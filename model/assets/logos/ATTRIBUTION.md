## The lettering font

The plain words on the plates (MICROCOMPUTER SYSTEM, VIDEO DISPLAY) are set in **Michroma** (`model/assets/fonts/Michroma-Regular.ttf`, Copyright 2011 The Michroma Project Authors, SIL Open Font License 1.1, `model/assets/fonts/OFL.txt`), from https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/michroma: an open, wide extended sans close to the plates' original lettering (a reference photo of the name plate). Downloaded 2026-11-12.
